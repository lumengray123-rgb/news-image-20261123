## 1. Kyiv claims its sea drones sank a Russian warship off Libya (LEAD)
- President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday that Ukraine sank the Russian
  Navy corvette Merkury — one of Russia's newest ships, commissioned in 2023
  — off the Libyan coast using seven Ukrainian-made naval drones. He said a
  Ka-27 military helicopter aboard the corvette was also destroyed, and a
  second target, the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which Kyiv says Moscow
  used to carry weapons, was hit. The ship had been struck while docked at
  the Benghazi naval base the day before; Zelenskyy's post was the first
  official Ukrainian acknowledgement of the attack, about 2,400 km from the
  front line. Russia has not confirmed the loss.
- Sources: Reuters (23 Nov 2026, "Ukraine says it sank Russian warship, hit
  cargo ship in naval drone attack off Libya"); Associated Press (23 Nov
  2026, via Netscape/Glasgow Times); ABC News (23 Nov 2026).

## 2. Iran vows to defy the U.S. naval blockade; U.S. hits its shadow banking
- Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said on Monday that
  Tehran would use "all available tools" to confront the U.S. naval blockade
  of Iranian oil exports. On the same day the U.S. Treasury announced
  sanctions on more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses, front companies
  and individuals accused of running Iran's shadow banking networks, which
  Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called the regime's "last financial
  lifeline". FinCEN flagged about $14.7 billion in Iran-linked transactions
  between January 2025 and August 2026.
- Sources: Reuters (23 Nov 2026, "Iran says it will use all available tools
  to confront US naval blockade"); U.S. Treasury / Al Jazeera (23 Nov 2026,
  "US escalates sanctions on Iran, targets 'shadow banking' networks");
  Cumhuriyet (23 Nov 2026).

## 3. EU unveils 22nd sanctions package against Russia
- European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen presented the EU's 22nd
  package of sanctions against Russia on Monday: a transaction ban extended
  to about 60 Russian banks, all to be cut off from SWIFT; a crackdown on
  crypto and payment networks, with a new Crypto Sanctions Task Force; more
  than 70 more shadow-fleet ships listed and a ban on Russian-insured
  tankers; a ban on Russian teams and athletes competing under the Russian
  flag at sports events in the EU; and a visa ban for Russian ex-combatants.
  The package still needs unanimous approval by EU member states.
- Sources: European Commission statement (23 Nov 2026); Reuters (23 Nov
  2026, "EU Commission proposes ban on Russian athletes, ex-fighters in new
  Russia sanctions package"); MLex (23 Nov 2026).

## 4. Indonesia factory fire: 24 killed, about 30 still missing
- Indonesia confirmed on Monday that 24 people died in the fire that tore
  through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery factory in Cikande, Banten,
  on Saturday evening, as rescuers searched the burned wreckage for about 30
  people still missing. A presidential labour adviser said safety protocols
  were being assessed and he would summon the factory's chief executive;
  preliminary reports pointed to an air-conditioner short circuit. Relatives
  waited at a police hospital while DNA sampling was under way to identify
  victims.
- Sources: Reuters (23 Nov 2026, via BusinessWorld / TBS News); Associated
  Press (23 Nov 2026, via Finwire).

## 5. Nvidia brings Jensen Huang back as CEO; shares jump about 30%
- Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as president,
  CEO and board director on Monday, effective immediately, eleven days after
  he resigned amid reports of an affair with a woman alleged to have worked
  for Chinese intelligence. The board said input from senior U.S. government
  officials left it confident his return posed no national security risk.
  Nvidia shares jumped about 30% in premarket trading and roughly 33% by
  mid-morning, lifting the Nasdaq about 7% and the S&P 500 about 5%.
- Sources: Reuters (23 Nov 2026); The Ledger Asia (23 Nov 2026); TBS News
  (23 Nov 2026); Nvidia statement / SEC filing (23 Nov 2026).

## 6. Armenia publishes draft constitution in step to peace with Azerbaijan
- Armenia published a draft proposal of a new constitution on Monday, a key
  step towards signing a formal peace agreement with Azerbaijan. The draft
  rewrites the preamble and removes the reference to Nagorno-Karabakh that
  Baku has long objected to. Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan said in an
  address to the nation that after the new constitution is adopted, Armenia
  would sign and ratify a peace agreement, giving "institutional, lasting
  and irreversible peace". He pledged to resign if voters reject it in a
  referendum.
- Sources: Reuters (23 Nov 2026, "Armenia proposes new constitution in step
  towards peace with Azerbaijan"); Associated Press (23 Nov 2026, via
  Reflector / Netscape).

## 7. U.S. drone helped Mexico catch "El Chapo Isidro"; 20 dead in violence
- Mexican officials said on Monday that the United States flew a surveillance
  drone over Mexican territory to help track down and capture Fausto Isidro
  Meza Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro", one of the FBI's ten most wanted
  fugitives, in Guasave, Sinaloa, over the weekend. The capture set off
  cartel violence that left 20 people dead — four soldiers and 16 suspected
  cartel members — and eight more suspected gang members were arrested on
  Sunday after roadblocks and burning trucks swept Sinaloa.
- Sources: Associated Press (23 Nov 2026, via Military.com); NPR (23 Nov
  2026); CBS News (23 Nov 2026).
